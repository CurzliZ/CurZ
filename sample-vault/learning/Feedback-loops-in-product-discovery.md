# Feedback loops in product discovery

Product discovery is a learning process: make a prediction, gather evidence, and revise the next question. A customer interview can reveal a problem, while [[Evaluation data]] can show whether a proposed solution behaves as expected. Neither source substitutes for the other; their disagreement is a useful signal.
