# Roadmaps are hypotheses

A roadmap is a set of bets about what will matter and in what order. Dates can coordinate work, but they should not make uncertain outcomes look guaranteed. Keep the next decision visible and revisit it when [[Small experiments]] or new interviews change the evidence.
