# CurZ brief

Promise: "Your notes become a brain you can see, question, and trust."
Obsidian shows links. CurZ shows WHY ideas connect, what PROVES them, where they CLASH, and how thinking CHANGED.

Five signature ideas:
1. The Brain: living graph (WebGL). Edge layers: explicit [[links]] (solid), semantic similarity (dotted, BM25 fallback if no embeddings), reasoned typed relationships the user accepted (colored).
2. Proof Engine: every AI claim has verbatim quotes; backend verifies they exist in the note; edited-away quotes become Stale; unverifiable output is discarded.
3. Review Queue: AI proposes relations/tensions; user accepts/edits/dismisses/defers. "Tension", never "contradiction verdict". Show why both could be true.
4. Evolution: dated timeline of the user's own claims on a topic, with a cited AI summary of what changed.
5. Replay + Bridges + Gaps: time-slider replay of the graph growing; bridge notes between clusters; orphans/open questions.
Plus Trust Report: measured eval numbers (fabrication rate before verification, relation precision/recall, retrieval hit@k). Never hardcoded.

Nav (5 areas): Home, Notes, Brain, Review, Settings. Right "Lens" panel: Links / Evidence / Timeline.
